# Guitar One 🎸

**Guitar One** is an interactive, bilingual (**English / Español**) web-based guitar learning studio inspired by **Yousician** and the **Gibson App**.

## Core Features
- **Account Creation & Login Interface (`Acceso Privado`)**: Multi-profile account creation, login with full `Enter` key submission, skill-level & guitar-style onboarding, XP/streak/star tracking, and instant Bilingual (`EN` / `ES`) switching.
- **6-String Color-Coded Tab Highway & Real-Time Pitch Detection**:
  - Uses the **Web Audio API** to listen to your real acoustic or electric guitar through your microphone and score every note in real time.
  - **Practice "Wait for Note" Mode**: Pauses the scrolling highway on each note until you play the right string & fret.
  - **Auto-Synth Play-Along & Drum Backing**: Built-in synthesis for **Acoustic**, **Electric Clean**, and **Gibson Overdrive** tones so you can jam with or without a microphone.
  - **Interactive 15-Fret Live Neck**: Click any string/fret on screen or press keys `1–6` to trigger notes.
- **Pro Chromatic & Multi-Tuning Tuner**: Supports *Standard*, *Drop D*, *Half-Step Down (Eb)*, *Open G*, and *DADGAD* with cent-needle accuracy and playable reference string pegs.
- **5-Stage Structured Guitar Curriculum**: 12 built-in lessons and iconic riffs from open-string grooves and campfire chords to *Smoke on the Water*, *Seven Nation Army*, *Sunshine of Your Love*, *House of the Rising Sun*, and *Sweet Child O' Mine*.
- **Chords & Scales Fretboard Lab**: 18 essential chords with a **60-Second Chord Switching Speed Trainer**, plus a full 15-fret **Scale Explorer** (*Minor Pentatonic*, *Blues*, *Major Pentatonic*, *Dorian*, *Mixolydian*, *Harmonic Minor*, etc.) with ascending/descending playback.
- **Studio Pulse Metronome & Custom Tab Builder**: Tap tempo metronome (`4/4`, `3/4`, `6/8`, `5/4`) and a visual custom riff builder that launches directly onto the 6-string highway.
